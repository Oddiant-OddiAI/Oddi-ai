Privacy Policy

Updated 22 October 2026 · Version {{ version }}

Information ODDI handles

ODDI uses account details such as your name and email address to provide sign-in and account features. Depending on the features you use, ODDI also stores conversations, saved career memories, preferences, learning progress, and files you upload.

How your content is used

Your prompts, relevant conversation context, and files you attach may be sent to the AI provider selected for a response so it can generate that response. Google sign-in is handled by Google. When this deployment is configured for Google Drive storage or sync, account or chat data may also be stored in the connected Drive.

ODDI’s current deployment has no analytics, model-training, or crash-reporting integration configured. Those preference controls are saved, but they do not enable those services here. The request-logging preference controls ODDI application request logs where supported; hosting providers may keep separate operational logs.

Where data is stored

Storage depends on the active deployment. Account and conversation data may be stored in the configured database, account-scoped local files, or connected Google Drive. Browser-only chat mode stores chat history in that browser’s IndexedDB with a local-storage recovery copy. Library uploads use the deployment’s configured file storage.

Retention and deletion

Conversation retention can be set to 30 days, 90 days, 1 year, or Forever. Expired active and archived conversations are permanently removed by scheduled cleanup; browser-only chats are removed the next time ODDI opens in that browser. A scheduled cleanup may take up to a day on server storage. Chats you put in Bin follow the separate 7-day Bin cleanup.

This setting covers conversation history only. It does not automatically remove saved memories, account settings, or Library files. Manage those separately in ODDI.

Your controls

You can change privacy and retention preferences in Settings, manage saved memories, delete conversations, and remove Library files through their controls. For a privacy question, use Help & Feedback inside ODDI.

This page describes the behavior of the current ODDI application. Storage providers and processing can vary with the deployment you are using.
